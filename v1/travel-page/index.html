Questions travel agencies ask

How do I manage travel enquiries from Instagram and WhatsApp in one place?

We connect both to a custom CRM, so each enquiry becomes a lead with a status, an owner and a follow-up reminder. You can see at a glance who still needs a reply.

Do I need Meta approval to connect WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. Connecting WhatsApp and Instagram needs Meta's business approval. We guide you through it as part of the setup.

Can I get a daily summary of my bookings?

Yes. The CRM can send one short Telegram or WhatsApp message each day with new enquiries, quotes waiting for a reply and bookings confirmed.
